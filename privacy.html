Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2025

Developed by: Ziad Khazzaka

Website: www.arabic-ocr.com

Contact: support@arabic-ocr.com

1. Data Collection

Arabic OCR does not collect or store any personal information. The app only processes images provided by the user to extract Arabic text and handwritten content using Google Cloud OCR API. We do not store your documents or any associated metadata after processing.

2. Use of Uploaded Images

All images captured or selected for OCR are temporarily sent to Google Cloud Vision API for processing. These images are used solely to generate text output and are not retained or stored by the app or its developers.

3. Camera Permissions

The app requests access to your device camera to allow capturing images for OCR purposes. This permission is required only to provide the app’s core functionality.

4. Analytics

We collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the performance and usability of the app. No personal information or document content is included in this data.

5. Data Security

We use secure and encrypted connections when transmitting data to Google Cloud. No uploaded content is stored by Arabic OCR after OCR processing is complete.

6. User Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there are no identifiable records to access, modify, or delete. Your usage remains fully private and anonymous.

7. Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Cloud Vision API to process Arabic text and handwritten content. Google may process the data as per their own privacy policies, which can be found on their official site.

8. Developer Information

Arabic OCR is developed by Ziad Khazzaka. For questions or support, please contact us at support@arabic-ocr.com or visit our website.
