Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract text from a PDF?

Open your PDF using the "Open" button in the top right. Once the document loads, navigate to the specific page and click the "OCR" button (eye icon) in the toolbar.

Can I edit or draw on my document?

Yes! Use the "Draw", "Highlight", or "Text" tools from the top toolbar to annotate the current page.

Is the PDF processed securely?

Yes, the PDF is processed securely. Using Apple Intelligence on macOS/iOS processes everything locally. On the web portal, files are securely handled in memory.

Does it support large documents?

Our Studio efficiently handles multi-page PDFs by generating thumbnails for easy navigation on the left pane.
